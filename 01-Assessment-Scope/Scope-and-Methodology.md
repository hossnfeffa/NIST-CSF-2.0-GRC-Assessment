# STS-GRC-001 — Assessment Scope and Methodology

**Organization:** Summit Technology Solutions (fictional MSP)  
**Period:** Four-week initial assessment, October 2026  
**Sponsor:** CEO Sarah Mitchell  
**Stakeholders:** IT Director Mark Reynolds; Security Manager David Chen; GRC Analyst  
**Boundary:** Internal corporate and MSP shared security operations: privileged access, remote management, endpoint visibility, monitoring, incident response, recovery, and governance. Individual client environments are excluded except where shared management access creates exposure.

## Objectives
Document Current and Target Organizational Profiles for selected CSF 2.0 outcomes, evaluate evidence, identify material gaps, assess risks, and propose a prioritized improvement plan.

## Rating convention (project-defined)
- **Achieved:** Evidence supports the outcome within the assessment scope and applicable review period.
- **Partially achieved:** Capabilities exist but significant elements or effective operation are unproven.
- **Not achieved:** Evidence supports the conclusion that the expected capability is absent.
- **Not verified:** Available evidence cannot support a reliable achievement conclusion.

## Risk convention (illustrative, not a NIST scoring standard)
Likelihood 1–5 × Impact 1–5. Low 1–4; Moderate 5–9; High 10–19; Critical 20–25. All numeric ratings require risk owner validation. Distinguish inherent from residual risk and record safeguards and evidence.

## Limitations
No comprehensive control testing, vulnerability scanning, client audit, penetration test or formal certification. Findings and evidence are simulated. Some conclusions remain provisional. NIST CSF 2.0 does not prescribe these ratings or risk bands.
