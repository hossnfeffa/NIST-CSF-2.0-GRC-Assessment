# Summit Technology Solutions — Executive Cybersecurity Assessment

**Framework:** NIST CSF 2.0  
**Assessment:** October 2026; four-week fictional case study  
**Status:** Initial selected-outcome assessment completed; remediation ongoing.

## Executive conclusion
Summit has foundational endpoint, monitoring, inventory, and security management capabilities. The evidence reviewed does not demonstrate consistent effectiveness across six selected outcomes. Key concerns are recovery testing, privileged access governance, inventory accuracy, incident response exercises, and monitoring validation.

## Selected outcome summary
- **5 Partially achieved:** GV.RR-02, ID.AM-01, PR.AA-05, DE.CM-01, RS.MA-01.
- **1 Not verified:** RC.RP-02.

These counts are **not** an enterprise-wide CSF compliance or maturity score.

## Material risks and progress
- Recovery assurance: provisional **20/25 Critical** pending actual restore testing and impact analysis.
- Privileged access: initial **20/25 Critical**; proposed **10/25 High** after authorized containment, pending review/alert validation.
- Asset inventory: provisional **12/25 High**; unreconciled records and ownership exceptions.
- Incident response: provisional **12/25 High**; exercises not evidenced.

## Decisions and actions
Leadership approves the proposed 90-day remediation roadmap in this fictional scenario, assigns risk owners, and requests monthly status reporting. Remediation evidence must be reviewed before findings are closed; unresolved residual risks require explicit decisions.

## External assurance language
Summit may state that it **uses NIST CSF 2.0 to inform its cybersecurity risk assessment and improvement program**, with scope and limitations disclosed. It must not claim NIST certification or comprehensive CSF compliance.

## Limitations
Simulated evidence, selected outcomes only, no comprehensive control testing or independent attestation. Provisional risk scores are illustrative and require owner validation.
